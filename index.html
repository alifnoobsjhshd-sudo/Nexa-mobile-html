Frequently Asked Questions

How is 6G different from 5G?
6G provides up to 100x the speed of 5G with virtually zero latency. It uses higher frequency bands (Terahertz) and satellite-mesh integration to provide 100% global coverage, even in the middle of the ocean.
Can I keep my current phone number?
Yes! You can port your existing number to NexaMobile seamlessly during the activation process. Our automated system handles the transition in less than 10 minutes with zero downtime.
Do I need a special SIM card?
NexaMobile uses advanced eSIM technology. You can activate your plan instantly by scanning a QR code or through our app. No physical SIM or waiting for mail required.
